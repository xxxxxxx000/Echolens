Built for Blind People and Low Vision Independence

Real time spatial vision and 3D audio for blind people.

EchoLens is an assistive vision system designed for blind people and individuals with visual impairments. It converts camera obstacles and GPS signals into binaural 3D sound cues with complete voice control and zero screen reliance.

👁️
Obstacle Awareness

Pans 3D sound cues to your left or right to avoid chairs, doors, and people.

🎙️
Complete Voice Control

No screen touches needed. Speak naturally to change settings or find objects.

📖
Instant Document Reader

Reads printed signs, menus, product barcodes, and colors aloud in seconds.

🧭
Audio GPS Orientation

Spoken live street geocoding and heading guidance for effortless navigation.
